# Code of conduct

Be kind, assume good intent, and keep discussion about the work.

Not acceptable: harassment, insults, discriminatory language, unwelcome sexual attention, publishing someone's private information, or sustained disruption. This applies to issues, pull requests, discussions and any other project space.

Maintainers may edit or remove content, and block people who don't follow this.

To report a problem, use GitHub's [report content](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam) feature, or contact a maintainer through their GitHub profile. Reports are handled as privately as possible.
